About the Pack

The Corgo Instrument Mega Pack is a comprehensive collection of instruments tailored for DefleMask software. With over 1,400+ files to explore, this pack offers a wide range of sounds in .dmp format, ensuring easy integration into Deflemask projects.

FM synthesis often results in instruments having a "sweet spot" key range where they perform best. Outside this range, the sound quality can diminish, making it beneficial to switch to a different tuning of the same instrument. This pack empowers developers to approach sound design differently—starting with a note melody and using Deflemask's real-time instrument switching to quickly "scrape" through instruments and discover the perfect match for their composition. Inglebard Gaming explains the issue within this video: The Inglebard Deflemask Tutorial Episode 2.
